## What is PoT-O?

PoT-O (Proof of Tensor Optimizations) is a blockchain consensus mechanism that validates tensor optimization proofs on a HexChain lattice structure with 30s target block time, 7 tokens with enforced supply caps, and ed25519 authenticated P2P transport.
